Request account deletion

For Chess99: Play & Learn Chess by AMEYEM, on Android and chess99.com.

You can request deletion of your Chess99 account and associated personal data without signing in or reinstalling the app. This is a support-assisted request, not an instant deletion.

Send your request

  1. Email support@chess99.com from the email address linked to your Chess99 account, with the subject Chess99 account deletion request.
  2. Include your Chess99 display name and say that you want your account and associated personal data deleted. For Google or Facebook sign-in, use the email linked to that account. A parent or guardian may request deletion for a child.
  3. Support will verify account ownership before processing the request and confirm completion by email. If you cannot access the linked email, contact support for help verifying ownership. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, card details or UPI PINs.
Compose deletion request

This button opens your email app; you must send the email to submit the request. If no email app opens, copy the address and subject above into your preferred email service.

What happens to your data?

The request covers your account/profile, social-login links, avatar, notification tokens, personal gameplay and learning records, chat and reports, and referral/ambassador information including mobile number and UPI ID. Support removes or anonymizes associated personal data. Completed games or tournament results may remain only after removing your personal identifiers.

Some transaction, tax, fraud-prevention or safety records may need to be retained for legal or security obligations. Support will explain any exception and its retention period when responding. Backup and third-party processing timelines will also be explained; opening this page does not erase backups or data held independently by Google or Facebook.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Support will tell you if verification or a legal obligation requires more time. Account access and saved progress are lost once deletion is complete. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account.

To request deletion of particular data without closing your account, describe it in an email to the same address. For Facebook-only data requests, use the subject “Facebook Data Deletion”.

Contact: support@chess99.com
