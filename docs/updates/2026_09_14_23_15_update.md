## Behavior now

| Game | Leave does server-side | Dialog copy |
|---|---|---|
| Rated (human or bot) | Resign, waiting at most `LEAVE_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS` (5 s) | "This is a rated game. If you leave now, you resign and it counts as a loss." |
| Casual vs human | Fire-and-forget `POST games/{id}/pause-navigation` | "Your game will be paused. You can resume it from Home within an hour." |
| Casual vs bot | Same fire-and-forget `pause-navigation` | Same pause/resume copy |

This supersedes the 19:50 decision that a casual bot leave sends nothing
(owner-directed). Fire-and-forget means the pause request runs on its own
`viewModelScope.launch` (not a child of the leaving coroutine), so navigation
never waits on it; failures are swallowed by `runCatching` and the server's
1 h pause window plus abandonment cleanup cover a lost request. Rated leave
keeps the 5 s bounded wait for the resign (it must be recorded as a loss).
