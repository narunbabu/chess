## Technique worth keeping (helpers were not added to the repo)
- Turn TalkBack on over adb: `settings put secure enabled_accessibility_services com.google.android.marvin.talkback/com.google.android.marvin.talkback.TalkBackService; settings put secure accessibility_enabled 1`.
  `settings put secure accessibility_enabled 0` turns it off.
- Swipe navigation without gestures: `input keyevent --alt 22` (next), `--alt 21` (previous), `--alt 66` (activate).
  Clear with `logcat -c` first, then read `logcat -d` for `Speaking fragment text` to get the spoken label.
- Under TalkBack, `input tap` is not a real touch-explore. The helpers used raw `sendevent` on `/dev/input/event2` instead: ABS_MT slot/tracking-id/X/Y scaled to 0..32767 over 1079x2399, then BTN_TOUCH down/up.
  One sequence focuses and two sequences activate. The device node and the scaling are specific to that AVD.
- If focus leaves the app, bring it back with `am start -n com.chess99.app/com.chess99.presentation.MainActivity`.
